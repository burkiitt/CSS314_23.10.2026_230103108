This practicum measures memory locality, loop order, false sharing, and process scaling on the profiled host. The stride and dense-kernel experiments show the cost of non-contiguous access, the false-sharing experiment compares unpadded, padded, and privatized counters, and the scaling study records strong/weak scaling with a simple roofline estimate. All generated result files are checked by `verify_outputs.py` and summarized in the signed verification receipt.
